Help and Support

Agri-Service is the app and web page for the people of Agri-Service, LLC, an AGCO and Fendt dealer with locations in Idaho, Washington and Oregon: your training, your certifications, lift plans, inspections, safety, HR and the company's news, in one place. It's powered by Prime Theory Labs LLC.

Step-by-step instructions for every screen, in the app and on the web page: signing in, the Centers, training and certifications, safety, HR, events and news, notifications, and what admins and owners can change.

Contact us

  • Your account, your location, your training records or certifications, or anything in the app: ask the training department.
  • A problem with the app or the web page: email Prime Theory Labs at support@primetheorylabs.com.
  • Your phone or computer itself: ask IT at it@agri-service.com.

Signing in

  • There's no sign-up. The training department creates every account and gives you the email address and password you sign in with.
  • The app sends no email, so there's no forgot-password link. Locked out? The training department resets it.
  • The first time you sign in to the app, it asks your name, your location and your groups. Your name and location are then locked; the training department changes them if they need to.
  • On the web page, at agri-barn.com, owners and admins are signed out after 30 minutes without using it.

Who sees what

  • Your training record and your certifications: you, your location's admin and the company's owners.
  • Certifications you add yourself (HR, My Certifications): a DOT medical card, a welding certification, CPR and first aid are seen by your location's admin and the owners too. Anything else you add, only you can see.
  • Safety Reports: who sent one is kept from everyone but the Safety Manager.

All of it, Apple Health in the Fitness Challenge included, is in the privacy policy.

When you leave

Your account is switched off: you're signed out of the app and the web page, and your phone stops getting notifications. What you did, such as lift plans, inspections and your training record, stays with Agri-Service as company records.
