Contact us

  • Getting in, a join slip or a password slip: your location admin.
  • Your name, location, role, training records or certifications, or anything in the app: your company's owners, or its Training Manager or Safety Manager for theirs.
  • A problem with the app or the web page: email Prime Theory Labs at support@primetheorylabs.com.
  • Your phone or computer, or your Microsoft password: your company's IT.

Signing in

  • There's no sign-up: your company puts you on its People list. You get in with a join slip from your location admin, a code with which you pick your own password, or, if your company uses Microsoft 365, with Continue with Microsoft, which appears once you type your work email.
  • No work email? You sign in with a sign-in name, your employee number and your company, like 004217@agri-service. Your join slip shows it.
  • The app sends no email, so there's no forgot-password link. Locked out? Your location admin gives you a password slip. If you sign in with Microsoft, your IT resets your password.
  • The first time you sign in to the app, it asks your name, your location and your groups. Your name and location are then locked; an owner changes them if they need to.
  • On the web page, at agri-barn.com, owners and admins are signed out after 30 minutes without using it.

Who sees what

  • Your training record and your certifications: you, your location's admin and the company's owners.
  • Certifications you add yourself (HR, My Certifications): a DOT medical card, a welding certification, CPR and first aid are seen by your location's admin and the owners too. Anything else you add, only you can see.
  • Safety Reports: who sent one is kept from everyone but the Safety Manager.

All of it, Apple Health in the Fitness Challenge included, is in the privacy policy.

When you leave

Your account is switched off: you're signed out of the app and the web page, and your phone stops getting notifications. What you did, such as lift plans, inspections and your training record, stays with your company as its records.
