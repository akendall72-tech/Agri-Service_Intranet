Agri-Service

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 13, 2026

Who we are

Agri-Service is the app and web page (agri-barn.com) that Agri-Service, LLC, an AGCO and Fendt dealer with locations in Idaho, Washington and Oregon, gives its own people. It's powered by Prime Theory Labs LLC ("we"), which builds and runs it for Agri-Service, LLC.

It's only for Agri-Service's staff. There's no sign-up: the training department creates each account. Agri-Service, LLC decides what its records hold and who in the company can see them.

What we collect

  • Your account: your name, work email address, location, the groups you work in, your role, and your UKG employee number, which matches you to your training records.
  • Your training: the courses and classes you've completed and their hours, classes you're registered for and your results, online course progress and scores, and Skills sign-offs.
  • Your certifications: your forklift certification and driving tests, your crane and rigging qualification, EPA 609, and any certifications you add yourself under HR, with their dates and the pictures you choose to add.
  • Your work in the app: lift plans, inspections, crane and vehicle checks, Safety Brief results and Safety Reports, support requests, special tool checkouts, your personal inventory of tools, the vehicles you drive and their service, events you sign up for, and what you post, comment or react to on Social. Photos you take or choose for these are saved with them.
  • Apple Health, only if you join the Fitness Challenge: your daily totals (steps, distance, flights climbed, your activity rings and their goals, exercise minutes and workouts) to score you on the company leaderboard. The app only reads from Apple Health; it never writes to it.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, a token from Apple that lets the app reach your phone, and which kinds you've switched on. The token is removed when you sign out.

We don't collect your location, your contacts, your browsing, or advertising identifiers. The app has no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools, and doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites. Nothing is sold.

How it's used

Only to run Agri-Service for the company: your training records and the AGCO dealer requirements, your certifications and when they expire, safety, lift plans and inspections, the company's news and events, and reminders and notifications about them.

Health information is used only to score the Fitness Challenge. It's never used for advertising or marketing, and never sold or shared outside the app.

Who can see it

  • You see your own records.
  • Your location's admin and the company's owners see what they need to manage the location: training records, certifications, lift plans, inspections and the like. A DOT medical card, welding certification, CPR or first aid that you add is seen by them too; any other certification you add, only you can see.
  • The Safety Manager is the only one who sees who sent a Safety Report.
  • Coworkers see what's meant to be shared: your posts and comments on Social, events you're going to, and, in the Fitness Challenge, your name, location and points. Nobody else, owners included, ever sees your daily health numbers.
  • Our service providers: Supabase stores the records and pictures and handles signing in. Apple distributes the app and delivers its notifications. GitHub hosts the web page's files. The web page loads its fonts from Google Fonts and some code from public code libraries (jsDelivr and cdnjs), which see your browser's internet address as any website would. They handle information only to provide those services.

We share information otherwise only if the law requires it.

How it's kept

Records are stored on Supabase's servers, and some are kept on your phone so the app works with no signal. Information travels encrypted, and access rules on the server limit each person to what their role allows. Pictures are stored privately and shown only to the people allowed to see them.

Your work records are Agri-Service, LLC's company records. When you leave, your account is switched off and you're signed out everywhere, but what you did, such as your training record, lift plans and inspections, stays with the company.

Your choices

  • Notifications: switch each kind on or off in the app's Settings, or turn them off in your phone's settings.
  • The Fitness Challenge: joining is up to you. Leave it and every day of yours is deleted. You can also turn off the app's access in the Health app.
  • Certifications you add: change or delete them, and their pictures, any time.
  • See, correct or delete your information: ask the training department, or email us (below). Records the company must keep, such as training and safety records, stay with Agri-Service, LLC.

Children

Agri-Service is for the company's employees and isn't directed to children under 13.
