Support Center

These are the cards in the app's Support Center. A saved change reaches a phone the next time someone opens that screen — no app update needed. Leave the person blank for a role nobody covers yet and the app shows "No one assigned yet" rather than a dead phone number. A role can hold as many people as it needs — Sales has two.

The icon is an SF Symbol name. If you type one this version of iOS doesn't have, the app falls back to a life ring instead of drawing nothing.

Announcement banners

These rotate across the top of the app's home screen and open full size when tapped. Three is the number the carousel is built around — more works, but people stop watching.

Image size: 1200 × 400 pixels (a 3:1 strip), JPEG or PNG, under 1 MB. Anything else is scaled to fill that shape, so a square image gets cropped top and bottom. Keep wording away from the edges.

Excel or CSV. The sheet needs a row with Payroll #, Pay Period Start, Pay Period End, Paid Holiday and Payday across it — the 2026 workbook already does. An import replaces the whole calendar rather than merging, so upload the full year each time.

Who does what

Drop Agri-Service Directory(Who Does What).csv here, or

Five columns in this order: Topic, Contact 1, Phone #, Contact 2, Phone #. A header row is skipped. Anything with an @ in the contact column is treated as a mailbox and gets an email button in the app instead of a call button. An import replaces the whole list.

Shared logins

The two sites the app signs into on everyone's behalf. Change a password here and every phone picks it up the next time that screen is opened — no new app build, nothing to reinstall.

These are not secrets. The app has to read a password to type it, and everyone in the company knows both already. What this gets you is being able to rotate one in a minute.

Shared reminders

The reminders every person gets. Changes reach phones the next time someone opens Reminders — no app update. People's own reminders aren't listed here and can't be changed from here.

Not showing takes a reminder off every phone and can be switched back. Delete is permanent, and anyone who had hidden that reminder loses the setting. Prefer not showing for anything that might come back, like a seasonal check.

Groups

The departments people put themselves in, and that support requests are aimed at. Renaming is safe — everyone in a group stays in it. Showing: No stops people choosing it but leaves everyone already in it where they are. Delete takes it out of everyone's groups for good.

Shops

The locations people choose when they first sign in. A shop decides which admin reviews someone's lift plans, and its time zone is what the Events tab uses when it hosts something.

A new shop shows up for people signing in for the first time. Moving someone who already has a shop is still done in Supabase, because the app locks it. A shop with people, lift plans or events can't be deleted — set Showing: No if it closes.

Add an event

Everything here shows in the Event Center on every phone, where people can add it to their own calendar. Enter times as the clock reads where the event is held — the app shows each person the time in their own zone, so a Twin Falls 9:00 AM reads 8:00 AM on a phone in Pasco.

Events
