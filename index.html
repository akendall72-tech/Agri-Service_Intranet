People

As an admin you see your own location's people; as an owner, everyone. Owners change roles here. An employee sees their own records; a location admin sees everyone at their location; an owner sees everyone and can reach this page. You can't change your own role, and the last owner can't be demoted — otherwise one wrong tap leaves nobody able to undo it.

Owners also set a name and a location here. Both are normally chosen by the person on first sign-in in the app, and after that both are locked: only an owner changes them, here. Anyone who only ever uses this page arrives with neither. Type over the name; pick a location from the list. These go through the same owner-only function deactivation does.

Also covers is for an admin filling in at another location, when one has lost its admin. Pick the location under their own and they're its admin too: its lift plans, inspections, Skills sign-offs, Training Status, special tools and Community, and the notifications that go with them. Their own home location doesn't change. Take it away with × when the gap is filled.

Employee # is what finds someone's training records. Each Training Status upload fills it in from All employees, by work email. Type it yourself only for someone who signs in with a different email from the one UKG has. Nobody can change their own, and no two accounts can have the same one.

Deactivate, don't delete. Deleting an account in Supabase is refused while it has lift plans, support requests or Skills history, so those records can't be lost with it. Only an account that never did anything can be deleted.

The email is the one they sign in with, shown here so an account with no name yet is still recognisable. It's read-only: change it in Supabase under Authentication → Users and it follows on its own.

Designations are for owners only: Safety Manager and Training Manager. They'll decide who can see or change things that belong to that job. Tick one to give it, untick it to take it away; your own row too. Anyone who isn't an owner can't hold one, and an owner made an admin or an employee loses theirs.

When someone leaves, an owner presses Deactivate. They can't sign in to the app or this page, they're signed out, and their notifications stop. Everything they did — lift plans, sign-offs, inspections — stays, with their name on it. Reactivate lets them back in with the same email and password.

Support Center

These are the cards in the app's Support Center. A saved change reaches a phone the next time someone opens that screen — no app update needed. Special tools

The list in the Support Center, in the app and on this page. Tools are added, changed and checked out there, one at a time; this is for the whole list at once — a copy to look over, or a big change made in a spreadsheet.

Drop the special tools list here, or

Columns are found by their headings: Part Number, Name, Description, Home Location and Quantity, then one column for each field below, headed with its name. Checked Out and Where in the download are there to read, and are ignored on the way back in.

The file is the whole list. Tools in it are added or updated by part number; tools not in it are removed. You see exactly what will change before anything is saved, and a tool that's checked out can't be removed or moved until it's checked back in.

Fields on the tool form

Anything beyond the name, part number, description, home location and quantity. A field added here is on the app's form, and on every tool, straight away — no app update. Answers are kept by field rather than by name, so renaming a field keeps them. Set Showing to No to stop asking without losing what's recorded.

Shared logins

The two sites the app signs into on everyone's behalf. Change a password here and every phone picks it up the next time that screen is opened — no new app build, nothing to reinstall.

These are not secrets. The app has to read a password to type it, and everyone in the company knows both already. What this gets you is being able to rotate one in a minute.

AGCO Technical Training Catalog

Drop AGCO's Technical Training Catalog here, or

Its LEVEL 1 to LEVEL 6 tabs are the courses you tick for each technician on the AGCO Development tab, with any added there. Upload a new copy when AGCO changes its courses — the ticks, and the courses added, are kept.

Courses added here

Courses that count toward a level but aren't on AGCO's catalog — the Level 3 online test-out, or a new class before AGCO lists it. They're ticked like any other. A course added to Level 1 or 2 is one more that everyone needs to complete that level.

Technicians

Everyone the Training Status roster calls a technician. The Training Manager opens one and ticks the courses they've completed; each tick saves, and their map changes with it. Other owners can look but not tick.

Level rules

What completes a level, where AGCO is likely to change it. Saving rebuilds every map straight away from the ticks.

Haystack labels

Everyone who reaches the flag gets a haystack under it, a bale for each Level 6 course they've completed. These are the words on the bales — a machine in a word or two. Leave one blank for the automatic label; a new Level 6 course gets one of those until you change it.

Training Status reports

Drop the reports here, or

Three UKG exports, all at once or one at a time — each is recognised by its columns, so the file names don't matter:

  • All employees — everyone's job and store. Technicians are the jobs with "Technician" in the title.
  • Credits All Time — every credit each person has earned: AGCO training hours toward the 24-hour credit for this year, and EPA 609. (The older Credit Rules Report still works, matched by name.)
  • OLT and ILT — course completions, for the history by location.

People are matched across the three by their Employee Number, then by email, and their records are kept under that number. Uploading also gives each app account its employee number, by work email, so People shows it.

Nothing changes until you press Publish. Each report replaces the last copy of that report; the others stay as they are. Admins and owners see the result under Training Center → Training Status, in the app and on this page.

No export to hand? Start from a blank sheet with the right columns — the importer reads it exactly as it reads UKG's.

Groups

The departments people put themselves in, and that support requests are aimed at. Renaming is safe — everyone in a group stays in it. Showing: No stops people choosing it but leaves everyone already in it where they are. Delete takes it out of everyone's groups for good.

Locations

The locations people choose when they first sign in. A location decides which admin reviews someone's lift plans, and its time zone is what the Events tab uses when it hosts something.

A new location shows up for people signing in for the first time. Moving someone who already has a location is still done in Supabase, because the app locks it. A location with people, lift plans or events can't be deleted — set Showing: No if it closes.

Add an event

Everything here shows on the Events tab on every phone, where people can add it to their own calendar. Owners can also add, change and delete events in the app. Enter times as the clock reads where the event is held — the app shows each person the time in their own zone, so a Twin Falls 9:00 AM reads 8:00 AM on a phone in Pasco.

Who's going shows on the event's page in the Event Center and the app. Owners add and take off people there. Switching back to No sign-up hides the list without emptying it.

Paste the Teams link. The app shows a Join the meeting button and puts the link in the calendar entry, so it is tappable at two minutes to the hour. Leave it empty if the link isn't made yet — the event still says it's online, which is what stops somebody driving to a location.

Events
