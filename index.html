People

As an admin you see your own shop's people; as an owner, everyone. Owners change roles here. An employee sees their own records; a location admin sees everyone at their shop; an owner sees everyone and can reach this page. You can't change your own role, and the last owner can't be demoted — otherwise one wrong tap leaves nobody able to undo it.

Owners also set a name and a shop here. Both are normally chosen by the person on first sign-in in the app — so anyone who only ever uses this page arrives with neither, and there was nowhere to fill them in. Type over the name; pick a shop from the list. The app still blocks both from the client so nobody can promote themselves or move shops to route their own lift plans elsewhere; these go through the same owner-only function deactivation does.

The email is the one they sign in with, shown here so an account with no name yet is still recognisable. It's read-only: change it in Supabase under Authentication → Users and it follows on its own.

When someone leaves, an owner presses Deactivate. They can't sign in to the app or this page, they're signed out, and their notifications stop. Everything they did — lift plans, sign-offs, inspections — stays, with their name on it. Reactivate lets them back in with the same email and password.

Special tools

The list in the app's Support Center. Tools are added, changed and checked out in the app; this is for the whole list at once — a copy to look over, or a big change made in a spreadsheet.

Drop the special tools list here, or

Columns are found by their headings: Part Number, Name, Description, Home Location and Quantity, then one column for each field below, headed with its name. Checked Out and Where in the download are there to read, and are ignored on the way back in.

The file is the whole list. Tools in it are added or updated by part number; tools not in it are removed. You see exactly what will change before anything is saved, and a tool that's checked out can't be removed or moved until it's checked back in.

Fields on the tool form

Anything beyond the name, part number, description, home shop and quantity. A field added here is on the app's form, and on every tool, straight away — no app update. Answers are kept by field rather than by name, so renaming a field keeps them. Set Showing to No to stop asking without losing what's recorded.

Open seat report

Drop the report files here, or

Three files, all at once or one at a time — each is recognised by its columns, so the names don't matter:

  • Open Seat Report CSV from UKG — the classes and their seats.
  • In-Class Event Report CSV from UKG — who's enrolled. Published together with the Open Seat Report, because each roster is matched to its class.
  • AGCO open seat report — the Technical Training Catalog workbook, or a copy with just its Open Seat Report sheet. Can be updated on its own.

Nothing changes in the app until you press Publish. Publishing replaces the previous copy. Classes that have finished drop off the report on their own.

No export to hand? Start from a blank sheet with the right columns — the importer reads it exactly as it reads UKG's.

Training Status reports

Drop the reports here, or

Three UKG exports, all at once or one at a time — each is recognised by its columns, so the file names don't matter:

  • All employees — everyone's job and store. Technicians are the jobs with "Technician" in the title.
  • Credits All Time — every credit each person has earned: AGCO training hours toward the 24-hour credit for this year, and EPA 609. Matched to people by email. (The older Credit Rules Report still works, matched by name.)
  • OLT and ILT — course completions, for the history by location.

Nothing changes until you press Publish. Each report replaces the last copy of that report; the others stay as they are. Admins and owners see the result under Training Center → Training Status, in the app and on this page.

No export to hand? Start from a blank sheet with the right columns — the importer reads it exactly as it reads UKG's.

Groups

The departments people put themselves in, and that support requests are aimed at. Renaming is safe — everyone in a group stays in it. Showing: No stops people choosing it but leaves everyone already in it where they are. Delete takes it out of everyone's groups for good.

Shops

The locations people choose when they first sign in. A shop decides which admin reviews someone's lift plans, and its time zone is what the Events tab uses when it hosts something.

A new shop shows up for people signing in for the first time. Moving someone who already has a shop is still done in Supabase, because the app locks it. A shop with people, lift plans or events can't be deleted — set Showing: No if it closes.

Add an event

Everything here shows in the Event Center on every phone, where people can add it to their own calendar. Enter times as the clock reads where the event is held — the app shows each person the time in their own zone, so a Twin Falls 9:00 AM reads 8:00 AM on a phone in Pasco.

Paste the Teams link. The app shows a Join the meeting button and puts the link in the calendar entry, so it is tappable at two minutes to the hour. Leave it empty if the link isn't made yet — the event still says it's online, which is what stops somebody driving to a shop.
